# Yue Liu's personal site

A single-page academic homepage, served by GitHub Pages from the `main` branch at
https://youngwing-0314.github.io. Pushing to `main` publishes it (allow a minute or two;
browsers cache pages for 10 minutes, so hard-refresh to check).

## How Yue works

- Yue is not a web developer. He describes changes in plain language, usually in Chinese. Reply in Chinese, casually and briefly. The site itself stays in English.
- He wants changes live without extra steps: commit to `main` and push, unless he asks for a pull request. Tell him when it is pushed and remind him to hard-refresh.
- After a visual change, send him a screenshot rather than describing it.

## Who it is for

Professors and PhD students deciding whether to reply to a research-position inquiry.
They skim. The page has to show, quickly, that Yue has built real robots on real hardware.
Keep the words sober and factual: no marketing language. The look is futuristic (Yue asked for a
holographic, Iron Man style interface in Nov 2026), but the text must stay easy to read and skim:
keep effects in the hero arm, hover and scroll transitions, never on the reading text itself.

## Files

- `index.html`: the whole site. HTML, CSS and one inline script in one file, no build step, no external JavaScript.
  The script draws the interactive wireframe arm in the hero (inverse kinematics on a canvas), plus scroll reveals,
  the section indicator in the top bar and the photo tilt. All content must still show with JavaScript off.
- `img/car.jpg`, `img/arm.jpg`: frames from the two YouTube demo videos. `img/board.jpg`: the lab board photo.
- `Yue_Liu_CV.pdf`: the CV linked from the page. Generated by `tools/make_cv.py` (needs `reportlab`). Edit the script and re-run it; do not edit the PDF by hand. It must stay one page.
- `_config.yml`: keeps this file and `tools/` out of the published site.

## Design

- Tokens live in the `:root` block at the top of the `<style>`: a dark-only holographic instrument panel. Cyan (`--holo`) for structure, links and the arm; amber (`--amber`) only for the availability line and the cube the arm carries. Style everything through the tokens.
- Type: IBM Plex Sans for headings and body, IBM Plex Mono for labels, metadata and readouts (Google Fonts).
- Avoid the generic AI-template look: no gradient text, no glassmorphism blobs, no purple, no emoji. Sharp corners, thin lines, corner brackets.
- Tab title: "Yue Liu · Robot perception and control". Top-left mark: "Yue Liu / robotics". The big heading stays his name.
- Figures are numbered in page order (Fig. 1, 2, 3). Renumber if one is added or removed.
- Check any change at desktop width, at about 400px wide, and with reduced motion (the arm should hold still).

## Content rules

- Every claim must come from Yue: the resume, the lab reports, or something he said. Do not invent metrics, results, or implementation details. If a detail is unknown, ask him.
- No phone number anywhere on the site or in the web CV. Email and GitHub only.
- The LCD, keypad and UART labs were done with a lab partner. Say so, and do not name the partner.
- Publications: link published work to its DOI. The IEEE Sensors Journal manuscript is listed as "submitted" until Yue confirms a newer status.
- Write in plain English, sentence case, no all-caps labels.
