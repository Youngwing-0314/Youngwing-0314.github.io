# Yue Liu's personal site

A single-page academic homepage, served by GitHub Pages from the `main` branch at
https://youngwing-0314.github.io. Pushing to `main` publishes it (allow a minute or two;
browsers cache pages for 10 minutes, so hard-refresh to check).

## Who it is for

Professors and PhD students deciding whether to reply to a research-position inquiry.
They skim. The page has to show, quickly, that Yue has built real robots on real hardware.
Keep it sober and factual: no marketing language, no decorative effects.

## Files

- `index.html`: the whole site. HTML and CSS in one file, no build step, no JavaScript.
- `img/car.jpg`, `img/arm.jpg`: frames from the two YouTube demo videos. `img/board.jpg`: the lab board photo.
- `Yue_Liu_CV.pdf`: the CV linked from the page. Generated by `tools/make_cv.py` (needs `reportlab`). Edit the script and re-run it; do not edit the PDF by hand. It must stay one page.
- `_config.yml`: keeps this file and `tools/` out of the published site.

## Design

- Tokens live in the `:root` block at the top of the `<style>`: solder-mask green accent on cool paper, with a full dark theme. Style everything through the tokens.
- Type: Bricolage Grotesque for headings and labels, Source Serif 4 for body text (Google Fonts).
- Figures are numbered in page order (Fig. 1, 2, 3). Renumber if one is added or removed.
- Check any change at desktop width, at about 400px wide, and in dark mode.

## Content rules

- Every claim must come from Yue: the resume, the lab reports, or something he said. Do not invent metrics, results, or implementation details. If a detail is unknown, ask him.
- No phone number anywhere on the site or in the web CV. Email and GitHub only.
- The LCD, keypad and UART labs were done with a lab partner. Say so, and do not name the partner.
- Publications: link published work to its DOI. The IEEE Sensors Journal manuscript is listed as "submitted" until Yue confirms a newer status.
- Write in plain English, sentence case, no all-caps labels.
